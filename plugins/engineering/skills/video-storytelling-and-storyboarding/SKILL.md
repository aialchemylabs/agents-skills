# Video Storytelling and Storyboarding

Create a production-ready story for the audience and intended viewing context. Resolve the message, intended action, duration, delivery formats, product facts and available assets. Make reasonable labelled assumptions for reversible choices; ask only when a missing answer changes the story materially.

## Ground the story

Inspect supplied product evidence, reference research and brand guidance. If external visual research is requested, use `design-reference-research` when available or inspect the relevant references directly. Keep sources and observed product behavior attached to claims. Do not present a concept animation as a recording of an implemented feature or invent traction, testimonials or outcomes.

Choose a narrative that fits the brief: demonstrate a task, explain a concept or introduce a change. Give each shot a purpose. Establish context, show the consequential action and result, and finish with an appropriate next step when the brief calls for one. Avoid forcing every video into a sales formula.

## Specify the sequence

Use [storyboard.md](assets/storyboard.md) for the handoff and [production-notes.md](references/production-notes.md) for timing, media and audio decisions. Include a script and concrete visual descriptions, not just “beautiful transition” or “cinematic.” Specify shot duration, visible content, action, transition, narration/caption timing, asset source and what the viewer should understand.

Check the complete duration and reading/speaking pace. Resolve time in frames for production, with a stated FPS and end-exclusive boundaries. Include relevant aspect-ratio adaptations: reframing, line breaks, safe areas and caption placement rather than simply cropping the landscape result.

An annotated sequence or shot table is a valid storyboard. Label placeholders, missing media and optional sound explicitly. Do not claim illustrated boards or an animatic were generated unless those artifacts exist. Storyboarder is an optional authoring application, not a prerequisite.

## Deliver for production

Provide the script, timed sequence, visual rules, asset/rights manifest, audio/caption plan, aspect-ratio changes and acceptance criteria. State what is already supplied, must be created, or remains unavailable. Use fictional demo data when recording production data is unnecessary.

If the user requested a finished video and authorized the direction choice, continue to production with reasons for the selection; do not stop for a redundant storyboard approval. For a planning-only request, hand off the storyboard without installing renderers, generating paid narration, or publishing. `programmatic-video-production` can execute the handoff when available; otherwise use the chosen project's existing renderer.
