# From story to an editable timeline

Ground each shot in the audience, message, intended action and supplied product facts. Choose a task demonstration, concept explanation or change introduction to fit the brief; do not force every video into a sales formula. Give concrete visible content, consequential action and result, narration/caption wording, transitions and relevant asset sources. Attach evidence to claims and label simulated UI, incomplete features and concepts.

Use the [storyboard template](../assets/storyboard.md) when useful. A script and annotated shot table are sufficient for planning-only work. List unavailable media and assumptions; do not imply that drawings, footage or an animatic have been made. Continue into production when a completed video is already authorized.

Use a stated FPS. Frames are integers; a shot covering `[from, to)` has `to - from` frames. Translate each duration deliberately and reconcile rounding against the total. Account for overlapping transitions once. Do not silently change the agreed duration to accommodate a script that is too long.

Check narration by reading it at a natural pace, preferably against a real supplied track. Leave time for the viewer to understand the result. Caption timing follows speech or the declared silent-video reading sequence. Keep full wording and language-specific line breaks available for accessibility and localization. A silent storyboard does not imply narration will be generated later.

For each asset record its source, usage permission/license, intended shot and local filename or pending status. Product screenshots/recordings must correspond to the described behavior. Supplied brand assets can define identity; external references inform direction without becoming a license to copy fonts, imagery, audio or logos. Keep private product captures out of public skill packages.

For real browser demos, use [recording.md](recording.md) when capturing footage. A storyboard requests observable interactions/results but cannot claim they have been recorded. After capture and editing, replace planned timings in the final coverage/caption/chapter handoff with measured export timings. Keep planned versus actual timing distinguishable.

For vertical and square variants, specify which elements move, resize, wrap or disappear. Preserve the primary action and readable copy. Include an early representative frame and a transition moment for review; select actual sample frames from the final video later.

Use [Storyboarder](https://github.com/wonderunit/storyboarder) only if its existing application/file workflow helps collaboration. A Markdown shot list, supplied sketches or a project-native storyboard may be sufficient. Do not install an application solely because a reference recommends it.
