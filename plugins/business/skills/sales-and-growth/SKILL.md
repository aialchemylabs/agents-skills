# Sales And Growth

Identify the target customer, problem, offer and evidence stage. A lead is not a qualified opportunity: record customer-confirmed pain, decision process, timing, alternatives and next agreed step. Use customer-discovery and proposal skills for their respective stages.

For a focused prospect shortlist, cold email or LinkedIn draft, call preparation, or objection rehearsal, use `cold-outreach` when available. Keep pipeline priorities and measurable growth experiments here.

For each growth experiment specify audience, channel, hypothesis, artifact, success metric, guardrail, duration and decision threshold. Separate observed funnel counts from estimates; show numerator, denominator and time window. Prepare outreach drafts grounded in publicly available or authorized relationship context. Sending messages or spending on campaigns requires an approved action.

Keep opportunity notes and commitments linked to source records. Review stale opportunities by their last meaningful customer signal. End with the highest-value next experiment or customer action and the evidence that would stop it.
