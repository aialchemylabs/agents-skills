## 2. Parties, Interests, and Issues

### Stakeholder map

| Party or constituent | Formal role | Decision power | Interests | Position | Constraints | Relationship | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Include people outside the room who can approve, veto, fund, implement, or undermine the agreement. In multiparty negotiations, map coalitions, side agreements, sequencing, and whether a tentative deal with one party constrains another.

### Issue map

| Issue | Our interest and priority | Their likely interest | Our target | Our limit | Objective criteria | Potential trade | Approver |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Label the other party's interests `known`, `reported`, or `hypothesis`. Translate positions without erasing them:

```text
Position: "We need 60% equity."
Possible interests to test: control, recognition, protection against under-contribution, or future dilution.
Questions: "Which decisions must you be able to protect?" "What contribution risk are you trying to solve?"
```

Do not treat a plausible explanation as motive evidence.

## 3. BATNA, Target, Reservation Package, and ZOPA

### BATNA quality test

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Specificity | What exactly happens if no agreement is reached? |
| Availability | Can it be chosen now, or does it depend on another uncommitted party? |
| Authority | Who can approve and execute it? |
| Economics | What cash, value, dilution, compensation, or opportunity cost follows? |
| Timing | How long until it produces the required outcome? |
| Risk | What could fail, and what is the downside? |
| Relationship | What reputational, team, or future-deal effect follows? |
| Reversibility | Can the decision be changed later? |

Compare alternatives on the same scope, time horizon, and risk basis. A vague hope, unaccepted offer, or bluff is not a BATNA.

### Decision boundaries

```text
BATNA:
BATNA evidence and owner:
Actions to improve BATNA:
Target package:
Target rationale:
Reservation package:
Reservation derivation:
Approval limits:
Walk-away triggers:
Counterpart BATNA hypothesis:
Counterpart reservation hypothesis:
ZOPA: supported estimate / unknown
Evidence that would update the range:
```

Derive reservation value from the credible BATNA, switching costs, timing, risk, and non-price interests. Do not set it merely by discounting the opening demand.

Treat ZOPA as an interval only when both reservation boundaries have sufficient evidence. Otherwise state that no reliable range is available and plan questions or offers that reveal preferences.

## 4. Objective Criteria and Value Creation

### Criteria test

| Proposed criterion | Source | Independent? | Comparable? | Current? | Geography/stage/role fit? | Agreed selection method? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use market data, precedent, professional standards, audited measures, cost estimates, neutral experts, or specialist-confirmed legal norms. Explain differences in scope, seniority, stage, control rights, risk, liquidity, and methodology.

### Option generation

Separate inventing from deciding:

1. list the parties' interests and differences in priority, risk tolerance, timing, forecasts, and capabilities;
2. generate trades, contingencies, sequencing, and joint fact-finding without commitment;
3. remove options that violate law, authority, ethics, the reservation package, or implementation reality;
4. assemble coherent packages;
5. compare the whole package with the BATNA.

Possible issue classes:

- money, equity, valuation, dilution, and payment;
- roles, authority, governance, board rights, and information;
- scope, volume, service, support, and timing;
- vesting, milestones, performance, review, and contingencies;
- risk, liability, guarantees, IP, data, exclusivity, and confidentiality;
- term, renewal, termination, exit, transition, and dispute handling;
- relationship, recognition, communication, and decision process.

### Multiple equivalent simultaneous offers

Use up to three packages only when:

- each is acceptable and roughly equivalent in value to the user;
- material differences are explicit;
- different terms test counterpart priorities;
- none is a decoy;
- every term is authorized or clearly contingent on approval.

| Package | Terms | Interests served | Value to us | Risk | Approval status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Conversation and Concession Plan

### Conversation architecture

```text
Purpose and relationship:
Agenda and decision process:
Opening questions:
Listening and summary checks:
Facts and criteria to share:
Information to protect:
Conditional disclosures:
Opening proposal or anchor:
Rationale:
Expected objections:
Questions behind each objection:
Trades and give-get rules:
Caucus or pause triggers:
Walk-away language:
Written recap and approval process:
```

Prefer open diagnostic questions:

- What problem must this term solve?
- Which of these issues matters most, and why?
- Who else must approve or implement this?
- What standard would both sides regard as legitimate?
- What would make the proposal workable?
- What risk are you unwilling or unable to hold?
- What happens on your side if no agreement is reached?

Do not ask for or encourage disclosure that would be unlawful or a breach of duty.

### Concession log

| Sequence | Requested move | Cost or risk to us | Required give | Authority | Expiry or condition | Agreed? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules:

- trade; do not concede unilaterally;
- use explicit conditional language;
- reduce concession size and slow pace;
- recalculate the whole package after material movement;
- avoid splitting the difference when it produces a package worse than the BATNA;
- do not bid against yourself after silence;
- pause when approval is required;
- record tentative items as a package and state that nothing is final until the agreed approval condition is met.

### Difficult behavior

When facing pressure, threats, anger, or an extreme demand:

1. pause and regulate pace;
2. acknowledge emotion or impact without validating unsupported facts;
3. restate the issue and objective criteria;
4. ask what interest or constraint drives the demand;
5. test an authorized trade or process change;
6. caucus, use a facilitator, escalate, or leave when authority, safety, ethics, or the reservation boundary requires it.

Never retaliate, shame, diagnose, or mirror deception.

## 6. Domain Prompts

### Cofounder

Ask:

- What contributions, time commitments, cash, IP, and opportunity costs are expected?
- Which decisions require individual, majority, supermajority, board, or external consent?
- How do vesting, role change, under-performance, leave, departure, death, disability, and deadlock work?
- How will information, pay, expenses, capital calls, dilution, and future financing work?
- What process repairs trust or resolves disputes?

Require corporate, tax, employment, and IP advice where applicable.

### Investor

Ask:

- What reconciled cash forecast, cash-floor date, financing need, milestone budget, and contingency support the amount and timing?
- How do valuation, instrument, dilution, liquidation economics, anti-dilution, pro rata, option pool, and future financing interact?
- Which board, voting, veto, information, founder-vesting, transfer, drag, tag, and exit rights affect control?
- What milestones, representations, covenants, or closing conditions create delivery risk?
- What signal or constraint will the terms create for later rounds?

Route missing cash, runway, and financing-need validation to `startup-finance-and-controls`. Require the accountable finance or legal owner to provide an updated cap-table and instrument-scenario model. Do not provide securities clearance; require qualified counsel.

### Hiring

Ask:

- What outcomes, authority, reporting, resources, and review criteria define the role?
- How do base, variable pay, equity, vesting, benefits, flexibility, start date, probation, notice, and exit interact?
- What internal-equity, budget, award, agreement, minimum-entitlement, discrimination, or pay-transparency constraints apply?

Require jurisdiction-specific employment review. Do not trade away statutory minimums.

### Vendor or Partner

Ask:

- What scope, service, quality, volume, dependencies, acceptance, support, payment, and change terms matter?
- Who controls IP, data, security, subcontracting, continuity, exclusivity, references, and customer contact?
- What governance, escalation, remedies, termination, transition, and exit assistance make performance durable?

### Difficult Stakeholder or Dispute

Ask:

- Which observations are agreed, disputed, or inferred?
- What impact, interest, fairness concern, identity issue, or decision-right conflict exists?
- What repair, future working rule, accountability, or escalation path is needed?
- Would a neutral facilitator, HR, board member, mediator, or counsel improve safety and legitimacy?

## 7. Agreement Implementation and Debrief

### Agreement-quality test

| Test | Question |
|---|---|
| Better than BATNA | Does the whole package beat the credible alternative after risk and timing? |
| Authority | Did every party act within authority and obtain required approvals? |
| Clarity | Are scope, money, timing, rights, duties, definitions, and exceptions unambiguous? |
| Incentives | Will the terms produce the intended behavior under upside and downside? |
| Dependencies | Are third-party, funding, data, staffing, and approval dependencies explicit? |
| Governance | Are owners, reporting, decisions, changes, disputes, and reviews defined? |
| Downside | What happens on delay, underperformance, departure, breach, or changed conditions? |
| Implementation | Who does what, by when, with what evidence? |

Use status precisely:

- `proposed`;
- `tentatively aligned`;
- `agreed in principle`;
- `approved`;
- `signed`;
- `implemented`;
- `verified`.

Do not treat silence, a handshake, an informal message, or a term sheet as final. Ask counsel about legal effect when material.

### Debrief

Record:

- actual interests and surprises;
- which assumptions were confirmed or rejected;
- offers, concessions, and objective criteria used;
- authority or process issues;
- package outcome compared with BATNA;
- relationship and implementation signals;
- follow-up owner, deadline, and proof;
- reusable lessons without retaining restricted information.

## 8. Research Basis

Apply and attribute these public sources without claiming affiliation:

- Program on Negotiation at Harvard Law School, [Principled Negotiation: Focus on Interests to Create Value](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/principled-negotiation-focus-interests-create-value/): separate people from the problem, focus on interests, invent options, use objective criteria, and accept only an agreement better than the BATNA.
- Program on Negotiation, [Negotiation Preparation Checklist](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/negotiation-preparation-checklist/): prepare goals, issues, BATNA, reservation point, aspiration, counterpart hypotheses, ZOPA, relationship, and objective benchmarks.
- Program on Negotiation, [When to Make the First Offer](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/batna/for-sellers-staying-mum-on-price-can-offer-hidden-advantages-nb/): decide whether to anchor only after assessing information and bargaining position.
- Program on Negotiation, [Multiple Equivalent Simultaneous Offers](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/conflict-resolution/dealing-with-an-uncooperative-counterpart/): use multiple acceptable, differently structured offers to discover priorities and create value.
- Program on Negotiation, [Ethics in Negotiations](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/dealing-with-difficult-people-daily/dealing-with-difficult-people-when-youre-tempted-to-deceive/): surface ethical pressure and reject deceptive claims about offers or alternatives.
- Program on Negotiation, [Agree on How You'll Disagree](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/dispute-resolution/in-contract-negotiations-agree-on-how-youll-disagree/): design dispute-prevention, escalation, and implementation mechanisms for durable agreements.
- US Securities and Exchange Commission, [Private Companies and the SEC](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/private-companies-sec): treat private-company investment interests as potentially regulated securities and require qualified securities counsel.
- Australian Fair Work Ombudsman, [About Employment Contracts](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/employment-contracts/about-employment-contracts): verify applicable minimum entitlements, awards, agreements, and employment law rather than assuming a negotiated contract can displace them.

Do not describe the skill as a Harvard method, Harvard-level, affiliated with, approved by, or endorsed by any source organization.
